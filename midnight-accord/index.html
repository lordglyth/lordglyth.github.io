What makes this world different

Bellwether Bay is governed by the Midnight Accord, a treaty between vampires, witches, shifters, and human witnesses. Humans who discover the hidden world are not automatically hunted: they are offered a tribunal, a memory seal, or a supernatural sponsor. Love is not illegal—but cross-faction bonds can wreck alliances, expose secrets, and make you politically dangerous.
