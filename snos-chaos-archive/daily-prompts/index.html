Sunday, October 18, 2026

7 prompts

  1. 01 Technology Mystery Solver

    Act as a detective for strange technology problems. I'll describe the symptoms, what changed recently, and what device I'm using. Give me three ranked explanations, the safest quick experiment for each, and a fix that avoids resetting or deleting anything unless truly necessary.

  2. 02 Invent a Creature That Could Exist

    Invent a bizarre but biologically plausible animal no one has discovered. Describe its anatomy, habitat, diet, courtship behavior, defense mechanisms, and one unexpected symbiotic relationship. Finish with a field researcher's first encounter.

  3. 03 Make My Chaos Into a Plan

    I'm going to dump an unorganized pile of ideas, errands, and unfinished projects. Group them by goal, distinguish urgent from optional, spot hidden dependencies, and give me the smallest doable first step for each. Don't discard ideas just because they're weird.

  4. 04 Build a No-Driving Micro-Adventure

    Design an unusual, low-stress half-day outing near North Brunswick, New Jersey that doesn't require me to drive. Prioritize accessible rest stops, interesting discoveries, realistic transport options, and avoiding crowds. Verify any current hours, prices, and transit details before recommending them.

  5. 05 One-File Weird Game Studio

    Make an original weird little browser game in one self-contained HTML file I can save and run offline. It must have clear instructions, mouse and touch controls, a real win-or-lose condition, a score, and replayability. Give me the full working code, not just an outline.

  6. 06 Obscure Knowledge Rabbit Hole

    Teach me something obscure and true about nature, history, space, food science, psychology, or engineering. Choose a subject most people have never heard of. Explain how it works with unforgettable comparisons, and distinguish verified facts from speculation.

  7. 07 Cinematic Continuity Director

    Turn a short fictional scene I give you into an eight-shot cinematic storyboard. Keep every character's appearance, props, location, injuries, lighting, and eyelines consistent across shots. Include framing, action, dialogue, sound, and the exact transition into the next shot.
