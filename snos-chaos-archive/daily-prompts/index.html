Daily dispatch

Monday, October 19, 2026

7 prompts

  1. 01 The Creature-Care Gameplay Laboratory

    Act as an indie game designer and invent a 15-minute gameplay loop for a cozy virtual-pet game about round, fluffy, tailless creatures that scoot by wiggling. The player's job is to feed, comfort, groom, play with, and bond with them. Create meaningful creature personalities, choices with consequences, persistent growth, three surprises, and a progression system that never wipes the player's beloved companions.

  2. 02 A Four-Voice Audio Drama Director

    Write a 4-to-5-minute original comedic fantasy audio-drama scene with four clearly distinct speaking characters, an absurd magical accident, and an ending that hooks the next episode. Format it for multi-voice production: label every speaker, give pronunciation guidance for invented names, mark sound effects and music separately, and include natural pauses without relying on a visual narrator.

  3. 03 Cryptid Case File: Evidence Versus Legend

    Choose a strange creature from folklore and investigate it like a curious but skeptical field researcher. Separate the oldest documented accounts, later embellishments, real animals that might explain sightings, and details that remain unexplained. Identify which claims need verification, cite reliable sources where possible, and end with a playful 'case remains open' field note.

  4. 04 The Freebie Treasure Hunter

    Find currently legitimate zero-cost digital discoveries I can actually claim today: free-to-keep PC games, time-limited ebooks, and open playtest or beta registrations. Use official or trustworthy sources, distinguish permanent freebies from demos and subscription offers, state deadlines and regional restrictions, and give direct claim or signup links. If a claim cannot be verified, leave it out.

  5. 05 The Ultimate Queso Nacho Engineer

    Design gloriously messy loaded nachos where abundant creamy white queso blanco soaks the tortilla chips until they're deliciously soft. Include guacamole, pico de gallo, and optional steak or chicken; absolutely no beans, refried beans, or ground beef. Give exact amounts for two people, a smooth queso method that avoids graininess, an assembly order for maximum sauciness, and a realistic shopping list.

  6. 06 The Android Feature Audit That Catches Everything

    Act as a demanding Android app QA lead. I'll paste a feature list or app specification. Turn it into a complete test matrix covering user flows, search, saved data, permission handling, offline behavior, error states, accessibility, screen rotation, and upgrade compatibility. Flag ambiguous or missing behaviors, write concrete pass/fail acceptance tests, and don't claim anything works unless it has actually been tested.

  7. 07 The Forbidden Spell Bureau

    Write a funny dark-fantasy scene in which a government department processes applications for newly invented spells. Make the magic rules internally consistent, feature one bureaucrat who takes the rules far too seriously, one brilliant witch who spots a loophole, and a seemingly harmless enchantment that produces a spectacularly ridiculous consequence. Build to a clever twist rather than random chaos.

Sunday, October 18, 2026

7 prompts

  1. 01 Technology Mystery Solver

    Act as a detective for strange technology problems. I'll describe the symptoms, what changed recently, and what device I'm using. Give me three ranked explanations, the safest quick experiment for each, and a fix that avoids resetting or deleting anything unless truly necessary.

  2. 02 Invent a Creature That Could Exist

    Invent a bizarre but biologically plausible animal no one has discovered. Describe its anatomy, habitat, diet, courtship behavior, defense mechanisms, and one unexpected symbiotic relationship. Finish with a field researcher's first encounter.

  3. 03 Make My Chaos Into a Plan

    I'm going to dump an unorganized pile of ideas, errands, and unfinished projects. Group them by goal, distinguish urgent from optional, spot hidden dependencies, and give me the smallest doable first step for each. Don't discard ideas just because they're weird.

  4. 04 Build a No-Driving Micro-Adventure

    Design an unusual, low-stress half-day outing near North Brunswick, New Jersey that doesn't require me to drive. Prioritize accessible rest stops, interesting discoveries, realistic transport options, and avoiding crowds. Verify any current hours, prices, and transit details before recommending them.

  5. 05 One-File Weird Game Studio

    Make an original weird little browser game in one self-contained HTML file I can save and run offline. It must have clear instructions, mouse and touch controls, a real win-or-lose condition, a score, and replayability. Give me the full working code, not just an outline.

  6. 06 Obscure Knowledge Rabbit Hole

    Teach me something obscure and true about nature, history, space, food science, psychology, or engineering. Choose a subject most people have never heard of. Explain how it works with unforgettable comparisons, and distinguish verified facts from speculation.

  7. 07 Cinematic Continuity Director

    Turn a short fictional scene I give you into an eight-shot cinematic storyboard. Keep every character's appearance, props, location, injuries, lighting, and eyelines consistent across shots. Include framing, action, dialogue, sound, and the exact transition into the next shot.
